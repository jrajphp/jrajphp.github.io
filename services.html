Teaching · Commissions

I teach. I take commissions.

The studio doesn't only make work — it shares the practice. I teach children and beginners the slow, careful joy of drawing on paper.

And the door is open for commissions. Almost anything on paper — I'm happy to talk it through.

01 —

Teaching kids

from the very basics

Hands-on classes for children and absolute beginners in watercolor, pencil sketching, and ink-pen drawing — starting from the very first stroke.

01 / 03

Watercolor

A gentle introduction to washes, wet-on-wet, and color mixing. We start with brushes, water, and one or two pigments — and learn to let the paper carry the work.

Format
In-person or online
Materials
Provided for in-person
Levels
Absolute beginner upward
02 / 03

Pencil sketching

Drawing what you see — lines, shapes, light, and shadow. We practice with a single grade of pencil, then expand to a full set, learning to slow down and observe.

Format
In-person or online
Materials
Sketchbook & pencils
Levels
Absolute beginner upward
03 / 03

Ink-pen drawing

Fineliner work — hatching, cross-hatching, and pattern. We build tone without erasing, learning to plan, breathe, and commit to a mark.

Format
In-person or online
Materials
0.3 / 0.5 fineliners
Levels
Older beginners (9+)
We start where you are — no experience required. Sessions are gentle and unhurried, single or small-group, with a clear plan for each week. Curious parents and absolute beginners are welcome too.
